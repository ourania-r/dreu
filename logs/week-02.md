## Approach and Implementation

I worked through the paper collection and organized it by LLM methodology rather than application domain, which surfaced six distinct research clusters: (1) LLM-assisted static analysis and cryptographic detection, (2) fine-tuned LLMs for secure code generation, (3) LLM benchmarking in cryptographic domains, (4) multi-agent LLM systems for security, (5) LLM-driven PQC hardware acceleration, and (6) quantum-safe software engineering frameworks, including Dr. Zhang's EST Lab work.

For each paper I captured structured notes on methodology, key findings, significance to PQC migration, and limitations. Recurring themes began to emerge across clusters: LLMs perform well at pattern-based crypto detection and contextual enrichment but fail at dynamic cryptographic reasoning; secure fine-tuning often trades away functional correctness; and no existing work evaluates LLM-based migration tooling at enterprise codebase scale.

The next deliverable also took shape this week: turning this understanding into a presentation for Dr. Zhang, to demonstrate my grasp of the field before moving into the research phase.
